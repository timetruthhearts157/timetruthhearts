Don't Dream It's Over

There is freedom within, there is freedom without

Try to catch the deluge in a paper cup

There's a battle ahead, many battles are lost

But you'll never see the end of the road while you're traveling with me

- Crowded House, Don't Dream It's Over

Life is beautiful and devastating.

The sight of the comet so close, blazing a path across the night sky, was the most ethereally beautiful thing I had ever seen.

The fact that it would destroy Earth by morning was a devastating blow that I hadn't quite reconciled just yet.

The comet itself would pass harmlessly by Earth in a dazzling display of the wonderous cosmos at work. The comets tail though carried a million pieces of rock and ice that would rain down on the Earth at a scale not even seen the day the dinosaurs were wiped from the planet. Nothing would survive as the cradle of Humanity passed through the tail. It would be the end in an instant.

Disaster movies like to portray these scenarios with images of mass hysteria. panic, and pandemonium. But as I wandered the streets trying to figure out what to do, everything was eeriely quiet. It was a surprising twist that when all the world is going to end, people chose to use those last moments in love and connection, not panic and destruction. There was no need to panic and hoard, no one was surviving this, not even the billionaires hold up in their private bunkers in New Zealand.
